# SILA — pre-presentation checklist (one page)

**The day before**
- [ ] Copy `dist/index.html` to the presentation laptop **and** a USB stick. Open it once from each.
- [ ] Record a 3-minute backup video of the pitch (screen recording of the guided demo) and keep it on the desktop and the USB stick.
- [ ] Rehearse with `PITCH.md` and a timer; aim for 2:45.

**30 minutes before**
- [ ] Browser: Chrome or Edge, up to date. Close every other tab and window.
- [ ] Turn off notifications: macOS Focus / Windows Do Not Disturb; mute Slack, mail, calendar pop-ups.
- [ ] Power connected; screen sleep and screen saver off; display mirroring or extended screen tested on the projector.
- [ ] Sound off (no system sounds); phone silent.
- [ ] Zoom level 100% (90% if the projector is 1280 px wide or less). Check the top bar is not wrapping awkwardly.

**Set up the two screens**
- [ ] Open `dist/index.html`. Click **Run guided demo** → *3-minute pitch* → **Reset to clean demo data** ✓ → don't start yet (Cancel).
- [ ] Open a **second tab** (the start dialog's "Open a second tab (ER board)" link). Sign in as **Mariam Al-Harbi — ER coordinator** if needed. Leave it on the ER dashboard.
- [ ] Arrange the two windows side by side (demo on the left, ER board on the right). Both readable from the back of the room.
- [ ] In the right tab, scroll so **Incoming patients** is visible.

**Language**
- [ ] Decide the presentation language. English: leave as is. Arabic: press **العربية** in the header of both tabs (remembered per device).
- [ ] If switching to Arabic for a moment at the end, test the toggle once now and switch back.

**Final 2 minutes**
- [ ] Left tab: **Run guided demo** → *3-minute pitch* → reset ✓ → **Start**. The panel says "Step 1 of 13".
- [ ] Right tab still shows the ER board, and shows the reset (5 incoming patients).
- [ ] Backup video open in a paused player, one click away.
- [ ] Water, clicker, timer.

**If anything goes wrong:** Reset demo → restart the pitch; or press **Skip** and keep talking; or play the video (see the backup plan in `PITCH.md`).
